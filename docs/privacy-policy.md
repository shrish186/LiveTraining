## What the app records

During a session you start, the app records:

- **Motion** from your phone, your Apple Watch or a Live Training leg sensor (acceleration and rotation).
- **Heart rate** from a Bluetooth heart-rate broadcast you connect (for example a WHOOP band or a chest strap).
- What you enter: sport, session type, where the sensor is worn, notes, and the marks you tap.
- For squad sessions: the player names the coach enters.

The app does not use your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers. It contains no advertising or
third-party analytics.

## Where it is stored

Sessions are stored **on your phone**. They leave it only if you enter a server address in Settings and upload them.
They then go to that server (your club's or ours), linked to the athlete ID you set.

## WHOOP

If you connect WHOOP, the server reads your recovery, sleep, cycle (strain), workout and profile data through WHOOP's
official API, with your permission (WHOOP's own sign-in screen). You can disconnect at any time in your WHOOP account
settings. We do not sell WHOOP data or share it with anyone else.

## What we use it for

Only to show you your training: live calls, session reports, load and trends, and to improve the accuracy of these
features. Live Training reports training load and fatigue. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose,
treat or predict any condition or injury.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal data. For a squad or academy, the coach who runs the squad sees that squad's sessions.
We share data with nobody else unless the law requires it.

## Your choices

- Long-press a session in the Sessions list to delete it from the phone, or delete the app to remove everything stored on the phone.
- Ask us at [email] for a copy of the data on our server, or to delete it. We answer within 30 days.

## Children

Players under 18 should use the app with a parent's or guardian's consent, or through their academy, which is
responsible for getting that consent.

## Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
